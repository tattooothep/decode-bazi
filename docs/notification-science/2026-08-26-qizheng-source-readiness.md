# 七政選時 source-readiness ledger

Status: **blocked for verdict, ranking, scheduler, and push**. The source preview may expose factual sky positions and source metadata only.

## Locked primary artifacts

The canonical artifact registry is `src/lib/astro/qizheng/electional-source-manifest.ts`.
It pins the public-domain Wikimedia Commons scans of `陽宅大成` booklets 7–16 by
canonical URL, byte size, page count, and SHA-256. Its canonical source digest is
`af7999aff8395b33bc73fa3c6821e3455715bc03d76f0959afddb1392a394bf2`.

Acquiring a scan is not a rule-pack approval. Every artifact remains
`pending_double_verification`; OCR is used only to locate candidate passages and
operative text is checked visually against the scan.

## Verified requirements from 選時造命二 (booklet 8)

- PDF pp. 7–8: burial and construction use different priority hierarchies;
  construction prioritizes mountain/facing and the relevant natal owner. A
  universal activity score would contradict the source.
- PDF pp. 8 and 12: establish `體` (site/direction/natal degrees) before `用`
  (candidate year/month/day/hour and celestial helpers). Favorable `用` must not
  rescue a failed `體`.
- PDF p. 8: `坐三煞` and `向太歲` are severe directional conflicts even when
  celestial factors are favorable.
- PDF p. 9: main structure, roof/ridge, move-in, directional repair, and opening
  mountain/establishing facing are distinct activities with distinct inputs.
- PDF p. 40: the same named pattern can help one natal owner and harm another;
  generic pattern-only verdicts are forbidden.
- PDF pp. 91–92: the text uses historical `刻/分` subdivisions and a multi-layer
  sub-hour election. It does not attest a direct conversion to modern civil
  minutes, timezone, DST, or local apparent-solar time.

## Verified requirements from 選時造命三·化曜 (booklet 9)

- PDF p. 7: the procedure calls for positions of Sun, Moon, five planets, and
  four calculated bodies at the exact candidate time, plus natal/event ruling
  degrees and trine/opposition relations.
- PDF p. 7: if adverse `仇難` and supportive `恩祿` both hit the relevant natal
  degrees, the supportive factor does not cancel the conflict. Conflict is
  checked first.
- PDF pp. 126–160: several palace/cycle methods are explicitly lineage-sensitive;
  the source rejects alternate forward/reverse methods. No cross-lineage fallback
  is allowed.
- PDF p. 143: a top configuration is a convergence of multiple named components
  at the mountain/facing direction, not a weighted average.
- PDF p. 145: visibility/timing varies seasonally and uses sunset `刻分`; this
  creates a location dependency but does not specify a modern geodetic/time model.
- PDF pp. 181–183: year, day, and hour mansion layers remain separate facts and
  cannot be collapsed into one score without an attested rule.

## Verified requirements from 修方催生二／救貧／催貴 (booklet 14)

- PDF pp. 3–4: fertility, wealth, and office/status are separate activity
  domains. They cannot share a generic score or a single significator set.
- PDF pp. 51–52: the wealth procedure requires its named day, natal financial
  significator, `祿馬/恩曜`, target direction, and relation to the natal degree
  before work begins. A partial match is not a complete election.
- PDF pp. 69–70: calculate the relevant natal rulers/degrees first, classify

  named aspect/arrival at direction, mountain/facing, and central palace across
  year, month, day, and hour. Activity-specific significators remain separate.
- PDF p. 49: five-element mitigation is conditional on an identified overcoming
  relation and mediating element. It is not a generic fallback that erases a veto.

## Minimum defensible precedence

The verified text supports only this requirements order:

1. Select the exact activity and its source-specific inputs.
2. Establish site/direction/natal `體` for every relevant person.
3. Reject explicit conflict/veto conditions.
4. Evaluate exact-time celestial and named supporting factors.
5. Require the activity-specific convergence; do not average unresolved conflicts.
6. Where the source requires physical work, never present timing as a substitute
   for correcting the physical condition itself.

This is not yet executable because the lookup tables, natal-degree construction,
historical time conversion, modern ephemeris reference frame, aspect/orb rules,
cross-rule mitigation precedence, and reproducible worked goldens are incomplete.
The reviewed `修方` volumes are also multi-system by construction, so they cannot
be silently presented as a separate Qizheng-only method.

## Production gates still required

- double-entry, page/cell-level transcription with image provenance;
- an explicit modern timezone/DST/local-solar/geodetic contract;
- a pinned astronomy and mansion reference-frame calibration;
- complete activity and household-input schemas;
- a conflict/mitigation precedence truth table;
- reproducible positive, veto, boundary, and cross-lineage negative goldens;
- an explicit permanent exclusion for diagnosis, treatment, fertility outcomes,
  wealth promises, and status promises;
- five independent final approvals on the exact rule pack and delivery diff.

Until every gate passes, Qizheng preference and payload schema stay hard-off, and
no Qizheng occurrence, scheduler, notification category, ranking, or verdict may
be created.
